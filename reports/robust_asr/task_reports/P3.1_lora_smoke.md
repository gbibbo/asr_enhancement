Section 5.1 mechanical decision (P3.2 will record this; P3.1 only emits the inputs):

- `SMOKE_PASS = (macro_wa_gain ≥ 0.005 AND clean_regression ≤ 0.010) OR (max_family_wa_gain ≥ 0.010 AND clean_regression ≤ 0.010)` → **false** (macro gain negative, clean regression 11.4× the threshold).
- `SMOKE_PARTIAL = NOT SMOKE_PASS AND max_family_wa_gain ≥ 0.010 AND clean_regression ≤ 0.020` → **false** (max family gain negative; clean regression > 0.020).
- ⇒ predicted Decision A = **SMOKE_FAIL** (Section 5.10 fallback path will set `Decision_B_lora_full.include_lora_in_router=false` and `claims_enabled.positive_lora=false` after P3 gate approval; P4 will be `SKIPPED_BY_DECISION_A`).

The negative gains are consistent with a deliberately tiny smoke recipe: 200 steps, on-the-fly training-time degradation families (`cafe_background`/`phone_call`/`muffled`/`far_field_room`) that are parameterized differently from the v3.4.7 eval-time families (`cafe_noise`/`phone_band`/`muffled_lowpass`/`far_field_room`), no curriculum, fixed LR after warmup. P3.1 is a contract-fulfillment task, not a quality bar; the decision rule is mechanical and will be invoked by P3.2.
