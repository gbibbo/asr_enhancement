## Update — P8.2-upstream-leakage-audit (re-HALT after upstream overlap detected)

The provenance repair above resolved the public-corpus / license /
attribution gap, but the disclosure that the upstream is LibriSpeech
`dev-clean` motivated an upstream-level disjointness check. Result:
the 5 demo upstream utterances and the 5 demo upstream speakers are
**5/5 present** in `librispeech_validation.parquet`,
`degradation_v1_id_eval.parquet`,
`degradation_v1_ood_param_eval.parquet`, and every per-family
`degradation_v1_<family>_{id,ood_param}.parquet` subset (see
`reports/robust_asr/demo/provenance_audit.md` "Overlap table" section).

A latent gap was also found: the manifest's `upstream_audio_id` uses
a directory-style format `librispeech/dev-clean/<spk>/<chap>/<utt>`
while locked manifests use the flat schema
`librispeech/dev-clean/<utt>`. A naive string-intersection would
silently report 0 overlap and miss the leakage. The audit normalized
both sides to the locked schema to expose the true overlap. The
manifest was NOT edited — the format mismatch is recorded as a
finding (F3) for a future scope-change to address.

Section 3 leakage rule 5 ("Demo examples may not be drawn from
locked evaluation sets") is **violated at the upstream level**. The
demo-side disjointness recorded by the existing leakage test
(`audio_id` `demo/...`, `speaker_id` `exNNN`, on-disk WAV
`audio_sha256`) remains intact, but it is insufficient to satisfy
the leakage guard once provenance discloses the upstream is
locked-eval material.
